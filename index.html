Solar Engineering Engine

Automated Rooftop Solar Pipeline

Replaces manual satellite hand-tracing with automated spatial polygon extraction and solar yield estimates.

🗺️

Footprint Area Extraction

Queries OSM Overpass API to extract building polygon vertices and computes exact area via the Shoelace formula instead of bounding boxes.

📐

Fire-Code Setback Inset

Insets perimeter boundaries by configurable safety distances (default 1.5m) and subtracts roof obstruction tags before sizing solar arrays.

⚡

Array Sizing & Payback Yield

Sizes DC array capacity, string inverter ratios (1.20 DC/AC), annual kWh yield, and simple financial payback years based on local grid tariffs.
